# Mike Marchitto — bio

Mike Marchitto is a designer. The portfolio homepage introduces him as: "Hi, I'm Mike."

Tagline: I design modern digital experiences for startups and global brands.

Background: From Chicago, where he studied graphic design at UIC, a program grounded in Swiss and International design principles. That foundation shaped his early approach to visual storytelling. After moving to Seattle, he transitioned into UX design and spent several years working across corporations, agencies, and startups, learning how research helps create more authentic user experiences. Based in Miami, he balances product design with AI-driven exploration and time outdoors cycling in the warm coastal environment.

The site positions him as a UX and product designer working across research, visual design, design systems, and shipping digital products.
