# Mike Marchitto — bio

Mike Marchitto is a designer. The portfolio homepage introduces him as: "Hi, I'm Mike."

Tagline: I design modern digital experiences for startups and global brands.

Background: From Chicago, where he studied graphic design at UIC, a school rooted in Swiss and International design principles. Those studies shaped early work in visual storytelling. After moving to Seattle, he transitioned into UX design, working across corporations, agencies, and startups. Now based in Miami, he balances work with AI-driven design exploration and time outdoors cycling.

The site positions him as a UX and product designer working across research, visual design, design systems, and shipping digital products.
