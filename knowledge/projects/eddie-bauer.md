# Eddie Bauer (case study)

Route: /projects/eddiebauer

Hero: "An ecommerce experience to modernize the brand." Subtitle: The Eddie Bauer Ecommerce platform design enhances product discovery, strengthens brand identity, and improves the overall shopping experience.

Tags: Ecommerce, Website, Cross Platform, Product Strategy, UX Design, Design System.

Overview situation: Eddie Bauer aimed to revamp ecommerce with new features and brand aesthetics within six months to enhance marketing and lead generation while strengthening long-term customer engagement.

The case study covers discovery (competitive audit, workshops), design system, UI exploration, custom system iconography, and product detail patterns for a cohesive shopping journey.

UI Exploration: Key components were designed utilizing the design system to bring wireframes to life so mockups were visually cohesive and aligned with brand values.

System Icons: Crafting a custom system icon library strengthened visual language, consistent interaction patterns, and a unified scalable asset package for development.
